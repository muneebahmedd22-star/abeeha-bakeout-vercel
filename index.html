Meet the Baker

The Heart & Soul Behind the Bakes

Abeeha's Bakeout shuru hua 2019 mein ek choti si home kitchen se, jahan baking sirf ek hobby nahi balki passion thi. Aaj, 500 se zyada khush customers aur saikron premium custom cakes ke baad bhi, har cake bilkul fresh, hygiene standards ke sath aur handmade tareeqe se tayyar kiya jata hai.

Humara mission simple hai: aapke special events ko mazeed sweet aur memorable banana. Flavour aur hygiene par koi compromise nahi, tabhi har bite mein milta hai sachha maza!
